# Knock Knock marketing kit

## Primary positioning

Knock Knock is a privacy-conscious visitor memory for Ring households. It counts everyone at the door, learns only from owner-approved identity decisions, and keeps an accountable visit history.

## Tagline

**Know who's at the door before the knock knock!**

## Short descriptions

### 25 words

Knock Knock turns Ring events into multi-person, user-verified visitor alerts and history while keeping uncertain matches generic and identity changes accountable.

### 50 words

Knock Knock is an Android companion for Ring households. It detects each person in a visit, suggests familiar profiles only when evidence is strong, prompts users to label unknown visitors, learns from owner-approved corrections, and preserves an immutable profile history with clear photo and visitor-log retention.

## Judge-facing differentiators

- Multi-person correctness: detect and crop each face before searching.
- Human authority: AI suggests; household users verify; the Ring owner approves canonical changes.
- Trust ramp: generic alerts during a 30-day learning period.
- Honest uncertainty: unknown, face-undetected, and `Possible match` are distinct states.
- Accountability: immutable profile modification history.
- Privacy boundary: no access control, demographic inference, or consequential automation.
- Demo resilience: official Ring adapter plus deterministic synthetic fallback.

## Audience

- Families coordinating arrivals, deliveries, and recurring visitors
- Caregivers reviewing household activity
- Shared households that need accountable visitor context
- Future small-business teams needing visitor and delivery history

## Calls to action

- “See how a three-person Ring visit becomes three independent, verifiable decisions.”
- “Watch Knock Knock learn without overclaiming identity.”
- “Explore the open-source, privacy-conscious Ring and AWS reference architecture.”

## Messaging guardrails

Never say “identifies everyone,” “guarantees identity,” “prevents crime,” or “securely unlocks the door.” Prefer “suggests,” “possible match,” “visitor awareness,” “user verified,” and “advisory.”
